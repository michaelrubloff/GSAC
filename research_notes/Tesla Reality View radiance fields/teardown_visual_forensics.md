Research date: 2026-11-22 (one day after FSD v15.0.1 / firmware 2026.38.6.11 began rolling out on 2026-11-21).

**How this was researched, and its limits:** Every attempt to fetch a full page failed because the host is blocked in this sandbox. That covers x.com, notateslaapp.com, driveteslacanada.ca, kucoin.com and primexbt.com. So every finding below comes from search-engine snippets and summaries of those pages, not from reading the pages directly. Quotes attributed to Musk, Elluswamy, Sidhu and others are as the search tools reproduced them. They should be checked against the original posts before anyone publishes them verbatim. The feature had been public for only about 24-36 hours, so the forensic record is very thin.

## 1. What have firmware hackers and teardown researchers found in firmware/app code about Reality View (shaders, asset formats, renderer, which chip renders it)?

### Takeaway
I found no firmware teardown of Reality View. No source turned up shader names, asset formats (.ply/.splat/.spz), SH coefficients, Gaussian-related strings or renderer identifiers for the v15 visualization, whether from greentheonly or any other dataminer. The only hardware-location evidence is Musk's own claim that it runs on half of one TRIP NPU on the AI4 FSD computer. If true, that places it on the neural accelerator, not on the infotainment Ryzen GPU, and it would come from a neural generator, not a game-engine rasterizer. Earlier teardowns show that the visualization before Reality View was a conventional game-engine renderer on the MCU: Godot, with an Unreal Engine version in development.

### Inferences
- Musk describes the output as generated video frames from an NPU-resident model. If that is right, the MCU would only composite or display a video stream, and there would be no splat asset files to find. A teardown would more likely show a neural-network blob for the TRIP NPU and a video or texture streaming path to the MCU, not .ply/.spz files or splat-rasterization shaders. That is a testable prediction for future datamining.
- The earlier Godot/Unreal stack drew explicit object meshes on the infotainment GPU. Reality View is described as replacing those "explicit signals and draw calls," which is consistent with the rendering moving off the game-engine path. Firmware should still be checked to see whether the Unreal binaries remain and are used for the non-Reality View modes.

### Gaps

## 2. What do expert observers (graphics, radiance-field and computer-vision people) say after examining footage? Splat signatures or something else?

### Takeaway
I found no named radiance-field or graphics researcher who has done a frame-level forensic analysis that claims splat signatures (floaters, anisotropic smearing, view-dependent blobs). The most prominent spatial-computing commentator, Bilawal Sidhu, describes it as a fully generated view from one end-to-end network with "no game engine." Tesla-focused explainers call it diffusion or mean-flow generation. The only splatting claim is a secondhand mention of an unnamed blogger who read it as "full-scene 3D Gaussian reconstruction," and later analysis contradicts that.

It fuses all the cameras into a fully generated 360 drone view trailing your car, with the route drawn right in. Feels a lot like GTA 6 in real life, except the camera doesn't follow you when you get out of the car." He framed it as "Tesla goes the opposite direction using no game engine at all" and said it "uses less compute than the old way - replacing all the explicit signals and draw calls with one end to end network." — [Bilawal Sidhu on X](https://x.com/bilawalsidhu/status/2124323235063816204); [Threads mirror](https://www.threads.com/@bilawal.ai/post/DgBs2U5iBtT/video-teslas-reality-view-handily-sets-a-new-standard-for-spatial-awareness-in/). Note that he does use splats elsewhere, e.g. a Scaniverse 3DGS post. — [Bilawal Sidhu on X (Scaniverse splat)](https://x.com/bilawalsidhu/status/2042069658363117654?lang=en)

### Inferences
- The expert consensus visible so far points to a learned 2D (or 2.5D-conditioned) generative video renderer, not an explicit radiance field. Sidhu's "fully generated" and "no game engine" framing fits this, and he would plausibly have said "splats" if he had seen them.
- Not a Tesla App's "pixels mapped onto a 3D stage, then generative fill" description and Musk's "conditional mean-flow model" are compatible. One plausible architecture is to reproject camera pixels using depth or occupancy into the virtual camera to get a conditioning image with holes, then have a generative model inpaint and refine it. That is a hybrid of "camera-image projection" and "2D generative video," not 3DGS. This is speculation.

### Gaps
- No frame-by-frame forensic breakdown by a named graphics or radiance-field expert was found.
- No owner-reported artifact catalogue (morphing cars, garbled sign text, flicker, temporal drift) was found yet. The feature is roughly a day old.

## 3. Real-time while driving, or offline? Can the camera move freely to novel viewpoints?

### Takeaway
Every source describes it as real-time and streaming while driving, not a post-drive replay. The camera is a virtual "drone" camera above and behind the car. Pinch-to-zoom and zoom presets are confirmed. Tesla's notes use the phrase "arbitrary virtual camera," but I found no evidence of free orbit or rotation to viewpoints far from the car, such as the opposite side of an occluder or street level far behind. So the key novel-view test for a genuine 3D reconstruction has not been demonstrated publicly.

### Cited Findings
- **Real-time and streaming (Tesla release notes as relayed):** "New real-time generative driving visualization that continuously streams a view from an arbitrary virtual camera situated above your vehicle, enabling you to see and control what FSD is planning to do." — [Tesla Super Intelligence (@TeslaSI) on X](https://x.com/TeslaSI/status/2124229676055040007); [Teslascope 2026.38.6.11](https://teslascope.com/software/2026.38.6.11)
- **Zoom controls:** "Customize your Self-Driving zoom preference. Pinch the visualization or select one of the presets in Controls > Self-Driving > Visualization Camera Zoom." — [Teslascope 2026.38.6.11 release notes](https://teslascope.com/software/2026.38.6.11); [Tesla North](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/21/tesla-software-update-2026-38-6-11-fsd-15-0-1/)
- **View position:** One outlet describes a "moveable, photorealistic, full-motion view of your car's surroundings from an imaginary virtual camera situated just above and behind you." No rotation controls are described. — [search summary of v15 coverage, e.g. Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/cars/tesla-fsd-v15-rollout/)
- **Layouts:** full-screen, map, Split View, and a quiet mode. A three-finger horizontal swipe cycles views. — [Basenor](https://www.basenor.com/blogs/news/tesla-update-2026-38-6-11); [Tesla North](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/21/tesla-software-update-2026-38-6-11-fsd-15-0-1/)
- **Sidhu's limitation note:** "the camera doesn't follow you when you get out of the car," i.e. the view is tied to the vehicle. — [Bilawal Sidhu on X](https://x.com/bilawalsidhu/status/2124323235063816204)
- **Temporal behavior per fan summary of Musk:** chunked generation of 8 frames at 36 fps, conditioning updates 18 times per second, and acknowledged latency from compute limits. — [The Tesla Newswire on X](https://x.com/TeslaNewswire/status/2124502660670009468); [Not a Tesla App summary of the same](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/5065/tesla-fsd-v15-rolls-out-with-incredible-new-reality-view-and-25x-better-safety-video)
- **Interactivity:** Musk said users can tap a point on the view to send the car there. — [JournalArta](https://journalarta.com/en/2026/11/22/tesla-fsd-15-reality-view-rollout/)
- **First night-drive footage** (early access, filmed 2026-11-21) is described as "looks like a drone overhead." — [YouTube Short](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/leFjnS3dvqw)

### Inferences
- A viewpoint limited to a chase cam above and behind the car, with zoom, is exactly what a 2D generative model trained on a fixed family of virtual-camera poses can produce. It does not require a persistent 3D scene. A real 3DGS or NeRF reconstruction would allow arbitrary orbiting, yet Tesla does not expose it. That is weak evidence against an explicit radiance field, or at least evidence that Tesla does not want to show off-distribution views.
- "Arbitrary virtual camera" in Tesla's wording could mean the model is conditioned on a camera pose, which is common in pose-conditioned video diffusion. That still does not imply an explicit 3D representation.

### Gaps
- No footage or report found of anyone rotating the camera 90-180 degrees, dropping to street level, or viewing occluded regions. That test would discriminate between the candidates.
- No measured latency (glass-to-glass) or frame-rate analysis from independent testers.

## 4. Claims that it is generative video / world-model output and not an explicit 3D representation

### Takeaway
Yes. This is the dominant account, and it comes directly from Musk and Tesla. Musk said Tesla has had a "~pixel-perfect world model since last year" for synthetic data, and that the in-car Reality View is "the (much) smaller version" of it, "a conditional mean-flow model, based on the recent sequence of Tesla Universal Vision Encoder." Tesla's release notes call it a "real-time generative driving visualization." Ashok Elluswamy says the display is produced by the same end-to-end model that drives. Tesla's separate "Generative Gaussian Splatting" research exists, but no source links it to Reality View.

### Cited Findings
- **Musk (X, ~2026-11-22, replying to @BLKMDL3 and @wholemars):** "In case anyone is curious: We've had a ~pixel-perfect world model since last year, which is critical for synthetic data generation. The (much) smaller version of that in the car is a conditional mean-flow model, based on the recent sequence of Tesla Universal Vision Encoder." This is the wording as reproduced by search summaries. Coverage disagrees on whether it was posted on Nov 21 or 22. — [as relayed by The Tesla Newswire](https://x.com/TeslaNewswire/status/2124502660670009468); [Ming on X](https://x.com/tslaming/status/2124474912161087636); [Bilawal Sidhu on X](https://x.com/bilawalsidhu/status/2124323235063816204)
- **Musk:** generated "by real-time AI video without the need for a game engine," running on half of one AI4 TRIP accelerator. — [News18a](https://english.news18a.com/news/english_316936.html); [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/tesla-ai4-trip-real-time-ai-video/)
- **Ashok Elluswamy (Tesla VP AI Software):** the new display is generated by the same end-to-end driving model that controls the vehicle. "The arrows show the turns FSD intends to take, the pins show its intent to park in a spot and so on." — [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/5065/tesla-fsd-v15-rolls-out-with-incredible-new-reality-view-and-25x-better-safety-video); [Inshorts](https://inshorts.com/en/news/new-tesla-reality-view-tells-driver-what-the-car-intends-to-do-1795321018000)
- **Possible tension between the two descriptions:** Elluswamy says it is "the same end-to-end model," while Musk says it is a "much smaller conditional mean-flow model based on" the vision encoder. These are reconcilable if the generator is a decoder head that shares the encoder, but neither has said so. — [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/5065/tesla-fsd-v15-rolls-out-with-incredible-new-reality-view-and-25x-better-safety-video); [Tesla Newswire](https://x.com/TeslaNewswire/status/2124502660670009468)
- **Terminology inconsistency:** fan summaries call it "diffusion," while Musk says "mean-flow." Mean-flow is a flow-matching-family, few-step generative method related to but distinct from classic DDPM diffusion. A search summary flagged the mismatch. — [Unite.AI / search summary](https://www.unite.ai/tesla-rolls-out-fsd-v15-with-reality-view-and-vision-upgrades/)
- **The vision encoder is shared** between FSD, Robotaxi and Optimus, and "trained on millions of internet videos." — [The Tesla Newswire changelog post](https://x.com/TeslaNewswire/status/2124274064306929670); [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/tesla-rolls-out-fsd-v15-with-reality-view-and-vision-upgrades/)
- **Tesla's separate Generative Gaussian Splatting (not shown to be Reality View):** Elluswamy described Tesla's "Generative Gaussian Splatting" (around 220 ms, no initialization, handles dynamic objects, trained jointly with the E2E model) in an Oct 2025 post and talk. — [Ashok Elluswamy on X, "Tesla's approach to Autonomy"](https://x.com/aelluswamy/status/1981644831790379245); secondary: [The Future3D explainer](https://www.thefuture3d.com/answers/tesla-gaussian-splatting/). An ICCV link could not be confirmed. Do not confuse this with Meta's unrelated "Generative Gaussian Splatting" paper ([katjaschwarz.github.io/ggs](https://katjaschwarz.github.io/ggs)) or the NeurIPS 2026 "TESLA" 4DGS paper, which has no apparent connection to Tesla Inc. ([NeurIPS poster](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/poster/152766)).
- **The ICCV-demo distinction:** Ming's explainer says Tesla demoed generative Gaussian splatting at ICCV but that it is "not the engine behind this view." — [Ming on X](https://x.com/tslaming/status/2124474912161087636)

### Inferences
- **Weight of evidence:** Tesla's own first-party technical statements say generative video model (mean-flow) on the NPU, conditioned on vision-encoder features, with no game engine. That mostly rules out an explicitly rendered 3DGS scene as the display path. Gaussians could still exist as an internal intermediate (e.g. the GGS work feeding the world model), but nothing public shows Reality View rasterizing splats.
- Musk calling it a distilled version of the "world model" ties Reality View to Tesla's neural world simulator, i.e. it is more like neural video synthesis than a reconstruction.

### Visual signatures that would discriminate between candidate techniques
The descriptions below come from general graphics and computer-vision knowledge, not from Tesla-specific sources. Use them as a checklist for analysing Reality View footage. Background on how 3DGS works: [Recent Advances in 3D Gaussian Splatting (arXiv 2403.11134)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.11134).

| Technique | Signatures in footage | Behaviour at novel or extreme viewpoints | Temporal behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|
| **3D Gaussian Splatting** | Soft, slightly fuzzy edges. Elongated, anisotropic elliptical "needle" or smear artifacts in under-observed areas. Semi-transparent floaters in the sky or air. View-dependent colour shifts (from spherical harmonics). Blobby fine geometry such as wires, poles and foliage. Popping or sorting flicker when splats reorder. | Quality degrades gracefully but visibly. Holes and stretched ellipsoids appear behind occluders and on unseen sides. The geometry is stable and parallax is consistent. | The static scene is perfectly consistent frame to frame. Dynamic objects need a 4D/deformation model and often ghost. |
| **NeRF (implicit field)** | Smoother, cloudier artifacts. "Fog" floaters. Blurry distant regions. No discrete ellipses. Too expensive for real-time on a half-NPU at this resolution without baking. | Consistent 3D parallax, blurrier where it was never observed. | Consistent for static content. |
| **Textured mesh / occupancy voxels (game engine, e.g. Godot/Unreal)** | Hard polygon edges. Repeated stock assets (identical car models or textures). Stair-stepped voxel surfaces. Shadow-map and LOD popping. Crisp, legible but generic signage. | Perfect geometric consistency from any angle. Hidden sides are rendered with plausible generic assets. | Rock-steady. Objects snap or pop as detections appear. |
| **2D generative video (diffusion / mean-flow world model)** | Photographic textures. Hallucinated detail: garbled or pseudo-text on signs and plates, changing car makes or colours, lane markings that morph. Fine structures dissolve or re-form. "Dreamlike" drift. | Only works within the trained camera-pose distribution (e.g. the chase cam). Extreme novel poses either cannot be selected or break down badly. Unseen regions are invented, not left as holes. | Slow drift, flicker or "boiling" textures. Objects change identity across occlusion. Chunk-boundary discontinuities (e.g. every 8 frames, if it is generated in 8-frame chunks). Lag relative to reality. |
| **Simple camera-image projection (surround-view / bowl stitching, depth-reprojected pixels)** | Stretched or smeared pixels on vertical objects ("flattened" cars lying on the ground plane). Visible seams and exposure mismatches between cameras. Holes or black regions where no camera sees. Ego-car blind spots. Text stays legible because the pixels are real. | Becomes heavily distorted as the virtual camera moves away from the real cameras. | Faithful to real-time camera content, with no invented detail. |

### Gaps
- No public frame-level analysis has applied this checklist to Reality View yet.
- The biggest open tests are: (a) whether sign text and licence plates are legible and correct (real pixels) or garbled and invented (generative); (b) whether occluded regions show holes (projection), stretched ellipses (3DGS) or plausible inventions (generative); (c) whether the scene drifts or changes identity over time; (d) whether the camera can be pushed far outside the chase-cam envelope. None of these were documented in the sources found.
- Musk's original post and its replies could not be read directly, so the exact quote wording should be checked against the original.
