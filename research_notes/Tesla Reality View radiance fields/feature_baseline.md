# Tesla FSD "Reality View": feature baseline (what it is, when/how it shipped, how Tesla describes it)

Research date: 2026-11-22. The feature began rolling out about 24 hours before these notes were written. All sources are from 2026-11-21 or 2026-11-22 unless stated otherwise.

**Method caveat (read first):** WebFetch was blocked for every Tesla-news host tried in this session (notateslaapp.com, driveteslacanada.ca, electrek.co, teslarati.com all returned "host not in allowed network domains"). Everything below therefore comes from search-engine result snippets and summaries, not from full-page reads. Wording marked "verbatim" is the text the search results quoted for a source. Before publication it should be checked against the original page or X post. The X post timestamps were decoded from the post IDs (Snowflake IDs) and are UTC.

## 1. Official name, firmware/FSD version, release-note wording, dates, surfaces

### Takeaway
"Tesla Reality View" is an official Tesla name. It is the headline feature of FSD (Supervised) v15, specifically v15.0.1, which ships in vehicle software 2026.38.6.11. The rollout to a first wave of customers began on 2026-11-21. Tesla's own wording calls it a "real-time generative driving visualization" that streams a view from an "arbitrary virtual camera situated above your vehicle." It is an in-car, center-screen feature. Nothing found describes it in the Tesla mobile app.

### Cited Findings

Electrek (2026-11-21) reportedly noted that Tesla's announcement said "Reality View" while the release notes said "Tesla Augmented Reality." — [Electrek, 2026-11-21](https://electrek.co/2026/11/21/tesla-fsd-v15-rollout-25x-safety-reality-view/). ### Gaps
- I could not read Tesla's own release-notes text directly, from the car or a primary capture, so the verbatim wording rests on reposts (@TeslaSI) and search snippets.
- I could not confirm the exact "Tesla Augmented Reality" wording, or when and whether the rename happened, beyond the Electrek report.
- I found no information on whether Reality View can be turned on without FSD engaged, or whether it is in the Tesla app.

## 2. Hardware and regions

### Takeaway
FSD v15, and with it Reality View, is going to AI4 (HW4) vehicles in the US first, and possibly Canada. Several outlets report HW3 is excluded, but Tesla has not formally said so. Nothing ties it to AI5, and there is no official word on China or Europe.

### Cited Findings
- Reports say the early rollout leaves HW3 cars out — [Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/tesla-starts-fsd-v15-rollout-to-early-testers-and-leaves-hw3-cars-out/); [bsinews.id, "Reality View and AI4 Support"](https://bsinews.id/en/tesla-fsd-v15-reality-view-rollout).
- HW3 cars run a cut-down "v14.3 Lite" FSD branch, and Tesla hasn't said whether they will get v15 — [Drive Tesla Canada (via search summary)](https://driveteslacanada.ca/news/tesla-reality-view/). Another source says Tesla did not specify HW3 versus HW4 order, which conflicts with the claim of AI4 exclusivity — [search summary of v15 coverage](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/21/tesla-fsd-v15-release-notes/).
- One outlet expects Reality View to be limited to HW4 cars with the newer AMD Ryzen infotainment processor. This is a prediction, not confirmed — [Drive Tesla Canada (via search summary)](https://driveteslacanada.ca/news/tesla-reality-view/).
- Musk says it runs on half of one TRIP accelerator on the AI4 chip, which implies AI4 hardware (see Section 3) — [news18a](https://english.news18a.com/news/english_316936.html); [KuCoin flash](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/tesla-fsd-v15-uses-half-of-ai4-chip-s-trip-accelerator-for-real-time-ai-video).
- The early rollout appears limited to recent Model Y cars in the US or Canada. No official v15 indication exists for China or Europe — [Basenor, "FSD V15 Is Rolling Out"](https://www.basenor.com/blogs/news/tesla-update-v15-0-1); [evselect.com.au, Australia explainer](https://evselect.com.au/articles/tesla-fsd-v15-rollout-australia-what-we-know).

### Inferences
- Musk's "half of one AI4 TRIP accelerator" statement is the strongest evidence that the feature targets AI4 compute.

### Gaps
- There is no official Tesla hardware/region matrix. Nothing mentions AI5, Cybertruck, or other models beyond "recent Model Y." The Ryzen-processor requirement is unconfirmed.

## 3. Official statements on how it is generated (Musk, Elluswamy, Tesla)

### Takeaway
Tesla people describe it as AI-generated video, not a game-engine or 3D render. Musk says it uses "real-time AI video generation (no gaming engine!)" running on half of one AI4 TRIP accelerator, and that it is a small "conditional mean-flow model" based on Tesla's Universal Vision Encoder (UVE). It is a much smaller version of a "~pixel-perfect world model" Tesla uses for synthetic data. Elluswamy says the same end-to-end model renders the view, using less compute than the old visualization. **I found no Tesla employee using "Gaussian," "splat," "NeRF," or "radiance field" about Reality View.** The words "generative," "world model," "AI video generation," "re-renders," and "conditional mean-flow" were used.

### Cited Findings
- **Musk, verbatim as quoted in search results (reply to @BLKMDL3 and @wholemars):** "In case anyone is curious: We've had a ~pixel-perfect world model since last year, which is critical for synthetic data generation. The (much) smaller version of that in the car is a conditional mean-flow model, based on the recent sequence of Tesla Universal Vision Encoder" — quoted in [The Tesla Newswire on X, 2026-11-22 13:58 UTC](https://x.com/TeslaNewswire/status/2124502660670009468) and [Ming (@tslaming) on X, 2026-11-22 12:07 UTC](https://x.com/tslaming/status/2124474912161087636). I could not get the URL of Musk's original post. It predates 2026-11-22 12:07 UTC.
- **Musk on compute and game engines:** He described it as "real-time AI video generation (no gaming engine!)" using half of one of the AI4 computer's TRIP accelerators. He also said it uses less compute than the old way by replacing explicit signals with one end-to-end network — [news18a, "Musk Says Real-World View Uses Only Half the Compute of One AI4 TRIP Accelerator"](https://english.news18a.com/news/english_316936.html); [KuCoin flash](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/tesla-fsd-v15-uses-half-of-ai4-chip-s-trip-accelerator-for-real-time-ai-video); [PrimeXBT](https://primexbt.com/news/teslas-reality-view-runs-ai-generated-video-on-half-an-ai4-trip-accelerator/); [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/tesla-ai4-trip-real-time-ai-video/). Outlets give his post date as 2026-11-21 or 2026-11-22.
- **Musk's other quotes:** "In FSD V15, the Tesla AI computer re-renders reality with chevrons in the sky for turns like a video game." He also said "It's like GTA," replying to Sawyer Merritt, and called it "Grand Theft Auto in real life" — [shattered.io](https://shattered.io/musk-fsd-v15-like-gta-reality-view-three-claims-unproven/); [ZeroHedge](https://www.zerohedge.com/technology/tesla-rolls-out-gta-style-reality-view-full-self-driving).
- **Elluswamy (paraphrased or partly quoted via secondary coverage):** The same end-to-end driving model that drives the car "also renders a realistic view of the world around the vehicle." The view "communicates the intent of the car." Comparing a side-by-side image, he said "the right side uses less compute than what the left used to take" — [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/5065/tesla-fsd-v15-rolls-out-with-incredible-new-reality-view-and-25x-better-safety-video); [Drive Tesla Canada](https://driveteslacanada.ca/news/tesla-reality-view/); [Ming on X, 2026-11-21 23:33 UTC](https://x.com/tslaming/status/2124285103434350913). Outlets disagree on his verb: "renders," "re-renders," or "generates."
- **Tap-to-go:** "You can tap any point on the reality revisualizer to make your car go there." Most outlets attribute this to Musk and one to Elluswamy, so the attribution is unresolved — [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/5065/tesla-fsd-v15-rolls-out-with-incredible-new-reality-view-and-25x-better-safety-video); [shattered.io](https://shattered.io/musk-fsd-v15-like-gta-reality-view-three-claims-unproven/); [eletric-vehicles.com](https://eletric-vehicles.com/tesla/tesla-starts-fsd-v15-rollout-adding-reality-view-and-speed-camera-slowdown/).
- **Earlier Tesla Gaussian-splatting work (context, not Reality View):** In October 2025, Elluswamy posted about Tesla's approach to autonomy, including that Tesla's "Generative Gaussian Splatting ... has great generalization, runs in ~220 ms, doesn't require initialization, can model dynamic objects." Commentary says this was demoed at ICCV 2025 — [@aelluswamy on X, 2025-10-24 08:52 UTC](https://x.com/aelluswamy/status/1981644831790379245); [Ming on X, 2026-11-22](https://x.com/tslaming/status/2124474912161087636).

### Inferences
- From Musk's own words, the official technical framing is a generative video model (flow/diffusion family), not explicit 3D reconstruction. As described, a "conditional mean-flow model" conditioned on vision-encoder features produces 2D frames from a virtual camera. Rasterizing a stored 3D Gaussian or radiance-field scene would be a different approach. Tesla did not say whether the model keeps any explicit 3D intermediate.
- "Arbitrary virtual camera," the release-note wording, implies the camera pose is a model input. Rendering novel views is a property shared with radiance-field methods, but Tesla does not attribute it to one.

### Gaps
- There is no Tesla engineering paper, blog post, or talk on Reality View. I could not retrieve Musk's original post URL or exact post time. I found no statement on resolution, frame rate, latency, or model size from Tesla (the parameter counts in Section 4 come from fan commentary).

## 5. What users see: on-screen content, viewpoint, liveness, artifacts, and difference from the old visualization

### Takeaway
It is a live, photorealistic-looking, third-person "drone" view trailing above the car. It is generated in real time while driving, not replayed afterward. It shows surrounding cars, lane markings, road paint, the blue planned path, turn chevrons or arrows "in the sky," and parking pins. Tesla says the camera is "arbitrary," but I found no evidence that users can freely orbit or zoom it. Early artifacts are anecdotal. It replaces the 2020-era wireframe/occupancy-style FSD visualization and its explicit object signals with one end-to-end generated view that Tesla says uses less compute.

### Cited Findings
- **Content.** It shows the planned path, arrows for planned turns, and pins where FSD wants to park — [search summary of Drive Tesla Canada / TeslaNorth coverage](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/21/tesla-fsd-v15-release-notes/). A YouTube Short ("FIRST Tesla FSD v15 night ride: Reality View looks like a drone overhead," 2026-11-21) shows a blue planned path and turn arrows once Self-Driving starts — [YouTube Short](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/leFjnS3dvqw).
- **Live, not replay.** The release notes say it "continuously streams" a view in "real-time." Musk calls it "real-time AI video generation" — [@TeslaSI](https://x.com/TeslaSI/status/2124229676055040007); [news18a](https://english.news18a.com/news/english_316936.html).
- **Viewpoint.** The notes say "an arbitrary virtual camera situated above your vehicle." Sidhu says it is a "360 drone view trailing your car" — [@TeslaSI](https://x.com/TeslaSI/status/2124229676055040007); [Bilawal Sidhu](https://x.com/bilawalsidhu/status/2124323235063816204). I found no owner reports of orbit or pinch-zoom controls.
- **Display modes.** Fullscreen visualization, map, split view, or quiet mode — [@TeslaSI](https://x.com/TeslaSI/status/2124229676055040007).
- **Interaction.** Users can tap a point to direct the car there. One report says taps did not change the selected parking destination. Another says picking a spot only worked after disengaging FSD — [Drive Tesla Canada / Not a Tesla App via search](https://driveteslacanada.ca/news/tesla-reality-view/); [Kingy.ai](https://kingy.ai/blog/tesla-fsd-v15-0-1-guide/).
- **Owner impressions.** Sawyer Merritt: "Tesla Reality View is even more mind-blowing in person than how it appears online" — [X, 2026-11-22 03:24 UTC](https://x.com/SawyerMerritt/status/2124343282347978961). Zack (@BLKMDL3): "Tesla Reality View is out of this world and so insanely cool" — [X, 2026-11-22 08:02 UTC](https://x.com/BLKMDL3/status/2124413163454255246). A reviewer called it "the best backup/360 camera money can buy" — [search summary](https://kingy.ai/blog/tesla-fsd-v15-0-1-guide/).
- **Artifacts and fidelity.** In one early owner drive, "worn lane arrows appear in the rendered street." Commentators read this as faithful rendering of real paint, but it is a single secondhand report — [Kingy.ai](https://kingy.ai/blog/tesla-fsd-v15-0-1-guide/). One analysis notes no independent structured tests of quality have been published — [shattered.io](https://shattered.io/musk-fsd-v15-like-gta-reality-view-three-claims-unproven/). Searches found no Reddit threads on hallucinations.
- **Versus the previous visualization.** It "replaces all of the explicit model signals with a single end-to-end visualization using less total compute" — [TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/21/tesla-fsd-v15-release-notes/). Elluswamy's side-by-side compares it to what "the left used to take" — [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/5065/tesla-fsd-v15-rolls-out-with-incredible-new-reality-view-and-25x-better-safety-video). Coverage calls the old one the 2020 wireframe display, and @tslaming says Reality View "makes the old visualization look like a cartoon," with the old visualization having "worked like a middleman" — [Ming on X, 2026-11-21](https://x.com/tslaming/status/2124285103434350913).

### Inferences
- The old view drew explicit perception outputs: detected objects as 3D assets, plus lanes and occupancy. The new one is generated directly from vision-encoder features. If so, objects are no longer separate symbolic signals, which helps explain both the compute claim and the risk of generated-image artifacts.

### Gaps
- There are no measured numbers for latency, frame rate, or resolution. There is no confirmed systematic artifact catalogue (ghosting, smearing, object hallucination). I found no confirmation of free viewpoint control. I could not view the videos myself, so content descriptions are secondhand.
