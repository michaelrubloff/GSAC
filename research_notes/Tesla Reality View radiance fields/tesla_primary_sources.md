## Q1. What Tesla presented about Gaussian splatting, neural reconstruction and the learned world simulator (ICCV 2025, CVPR 2023–2026, other talks)

### Takeaway
Tesla's clearest and most direct disclosure is Ashok Elluswamy's ICCV 2025 talk and the X article he posted with it on 2025-10-24, "Tesla's approach to Autonomy." In it he names a **"Generative Gaussian Splatting"** system. He says it reconstructs 3D scenes from fleet camera footage in about 220 ms, needs no initialization, models dynamic objects and "can be jointly trained with the end-to-end AI model." He presents it as a tool for **debugging and evaluation**. It sits alongside a separate learned "neural world simulator", which is a generative video model and not a splat renderer. In no Tesla primary source that I found is it described as an on-car or in-car visualization component.

### Inferences
- Tesla's Generative Gaussian Splatting, as Tesla describes it, is a **feed-forward / generative network that predicts 3DGS** from driving clips, rather than per-scene optimization. Tesla says it shares training with the end-to-end driving model. Tesla described it in the context of **debugging/interpretability and evaluation**, i.e. seeing what the model "understands" in 3D. It was not described as a shipped user-facing visualization. This is the strongest primary-source basis for any claim that Reality View could use splats, but Tesla did not make that link.
- Whether 220 ms is per scene, per clip or per frame, and on what hardware, was not stated in the excerpts I could see. 220 ms is about 4.5 Hz, below a smooth real-time display frame rate. That argues against it being the per-frame renderer for a live in-car view unless it has since been optimized (inference).
- The "neural world simulator" is a separate, action-conditioned **video-generation** model (pixels out, multi-camera). It is not a radiance-field renderer. Tesla appears to run both approaches in parallel: generative 3DGS for 3D reconstruction and generative video for closed-loop simulation.

## Q2. What AI Day 2021/2022 showed about NeRF-style neural rendering and 3D reconstruction

### Takeaway
At AI Day 2022 (2022-09-30), Tesla presented NeRF as a **research direction tied to the occupancy network**. It showed an "early result of a 3D reconstruction" from fleet data and stated two aims: to fold NeRF features into occupancy training and to use network outputs as NeRF input state. It also showed NeRF-based volumetric rendering for Optimus perception. Tesla framed this as offline/research work focused on 3D geometry, not photoreal RGB.

### Cited Findings (AI Day 2022; community Whisper transcript, not checked against the official video, and speaker attribution approximate)
- "Besides the voxels and the surface, we're also very excited about the recent breakthrough in Neural Radiance Field or NERF … We're looking into both incorporating some of the last NERF features into occupancy network training as well as using our network output as the input state for NERF." [VERBATIM-TESLA, transcript; occupancy section presented by Phil Duan] — [AI Day 2022 Whisper transcript (GitHub gist)](https://gist.github.com/L0rdCha0s/de22ae0c7e7a7a70b37ac9c1262e27e1)
- "As a matter of fact, Ashok is very excited about this. This has been his personal weekend project for a while." [VERBATIM-TESLA, transcript] — [gist](https://gist.github.com/L0rdCha0s/de22ae0c7e7a7a70b37ac9c1262e27e1)
- Probably Elluswamy speaking (transcript does not label the speaker): "for vision, NERFs are going to provide the foundation models for computer vision because they are grounded in geometry … the supervision is essentially free because you just have to differentially render these images … this occupancy network idea where images come in and then the network produces a consistent volumetric representation of the scene that can then be differentially rendered into any image that was observed. I personally think it's a future of computer vision." [VERBATIM-TESLA, transcript] — [gist](https://gist.github.com/L0rdCha0s/de22ae0c7e7a7a70b37ac9c1262e27e1)
- "So here's an example early result of a 3D reconstruction from our [fleet] data. Instead of focusing on getting perfect RGB reproduction in image space, our primary goal here is to accurately represent the world in 3D space for driving … for all our [fleet] data over the world in all weather and lighting conditions." (Whisper rendered "fleet" as "free"; corrected in brackets.) [VERBATIM-TESLA, transcript] — [gist](https://gist.github.com/L0rdCha0s/de22ae0c7e7a7a70b37ac9c1262e27e1)
- Optimus segment (Milan Kovac): "Using work made on [neural] radiance fields to get really great volumetric rendering of the bot's environments." [VERBATIM-TESLA, transcript] — [gist](https://gist.github.com/L0rdCha0s/de22ae0c7e7a7a70b37ac9c1262e27e1)
- Simulation at AI Day 2022 used the conventional "autopilot simulator" and "our simulation system to synthetically create images … we trivially have all the labels." These are not neural-rendering claims. [VERBATIM-TESLA, transcript] — [gist](https://gist.github.com/L0rdCha0s/de22ae0c7e7a7a70b37ac9c1262e27e1)
- Secondary analysis describes the NeRF use as offline: reconstructions serve as ground truth or supervision for occupancy, with fleet averaging used to keep scenes up to date. [MEDIA/analysis] — [42how](https://en.42how.com/2022/10/26/tesla-fsd-occupancy-network-explained/); [Analytics India Mag](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-trends/the-brilliant-language-of-lanes)

### Inferences
- There is a continuous line of thinking from 2022 to 2025. In 2022 the idea was "one-shot prediction of volumetric occupancy that can be differentially rendered," described as generalizing across scenes, unlike per-scene NeRF. By 2025 it had become "Generative Gaussian Splatting," which needs no initialization. Both are **feed-forward, cross-scene, geometry-first** reconstructions. That fits Elluswamy's long-stated personal interest (the "weekend project" remark).

### Gaps
- AI Day 2021 (2021-08-19): I did not fetch primary material. From memory, multi-trip road-surface reconstruction in the auto-labeling pipeline and a procedural (non-neural) simulator were shown, but this is unsourced here and needs checking. I found no evidence of NeRF/3DGS at AI Day 2021.

## Q4. Elon Musk's statements, and what is known about how Reality View (FSD v15) is generated

### Takeaway
FSD v15, with "Reality View," began rolling out on **2026-11-21**. By media accounts, Musk's public description is **real-time AI video generation, "not a game engine,"** running on roughly half of one AI4 TRIP accelerator. A social-media breakdown of Musk's remarks says the view is painted by a **separate diffusion model (~1B parameters) fed by shared vision-encoder features**, with a conditional mean-flow model in the car. That breakdown explicitly says the Gaussian splatting demo is **not** the Reality View engine. No Tesla primary source I found says Reality View uses Gaussian splatting. Claims that it does trace back to secondary posts that conflate it with the 2025 splatting talk.

### Cited Findings
- FSD v15 rollout began 2026-11-21. Reality View is a third-person live visualization showing planned actions: turn chevrons "in the sky," and tap-to-direct. Tesla says it replaces several separate model signals with one visualization that uses less total compute. [MEDIA summarizing Tesla release notes and Musk] — [Teslarati release notes](https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-full-self-driving-v15-rollout-begins-full-release-notes-improvements/); [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/tesla-rolls-out-fsd-v15-with-reality-view-and-vision-upgrades/); [Gizmodo "It's Like GTA"](https://gizmodo.com/its-like-gta-musk-touts-video-game-like-update-to-tesla-fsd-2000843584); [eletric-vehicles.com](https://eletric-vehicles.com/tesla/tesla-starts-fsd-v15-rollout-adding-reality-view-and-speed-camera-slowdown/)

### Inferences
- The best available evidence points to Reality View being a **2D generative (diffusion / mean-flow) image or video model conditioned on driving-network features**, i.e. neural rendering in the broad sense. It does not point to explicit 3D Gaussian splatting. This is consistent with Musk's "AI video generation, not a game engine" framing and with Tesla's existing video-generation world simulator. Tesla's own 220 ms latency figure for Generative GS is also too slow for a smooth per-frame display.
- It remains **plausible but unconfirmed** that Reality View draws on technology shared with the Generative GS work. Tesla said Generative GS is "jointly trained with the end-to-end AI model," and Reality View reportedly shares encoder features. A hybrid, such as a splat- or occupancy-based 3D scaffold with diffusion-painted appearance, cannot be ruled out from public information.
- Media and forum claims that "Reality View = Gaussian splatting" appear to be **conflations** of the 2025-10-24 ICCV disclosure with the 2026-11-21 launch. No Tesla source makes that link.

- Tesla has published no architecture diagram, paper or blog post for Reality View. No Tesla engineer statement on its rendering method was found.
