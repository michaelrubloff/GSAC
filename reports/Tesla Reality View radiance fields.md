# Tesla's Reality View points to Gaussian splats

> **Revised 2026-11-22. This replaces the earlier verdict ("paints pixels, not splats"), which was wrong.** Two X posts the editor supplied, and the Reality View demo video posted by a Tesla engineer, were read directly this time through an X mirror API. They are first-party evidence that outranks the search-snippet sourcing the first draft rested on. Together they point to **Gaussian splats as the scene representation behind Reality View**, most likely produced live by a generative, feed-forward model: Tesla's "Generative Gaussian Splatting" line of work. Tesla has still not said so in an official statement. The new evidence comes first below. The original analysis follows, kept for its sourcing, with its conclusions corrected.

4. **Tesla's self-driving lead.** Phil Duan (@pduan), whose bio reads **"Leading Self-Driving @Tesla"**, replied in the same thread: *"don't ever underestimate the heart of a gaussian splats champion"* ([post](https://x.com/pduan/status/2124356615344415033), 04:17 UTC). Duan presented the occupancy and NeRF material at AI Day 2022.

**The engineer's track record.** Neda's public GitHub matches the "splats champion" description. It includes `rust-gs` ("Gaussian Splat Viewer made from scratch using Rust and WebGL", last pushed 2026-07), `gaussian_semantics` ("Adding semantic information to Gaussian Splatting"), `NeRfFromScratch`, `gs-editor` and `worldmodel` (github.com/Shahanneda, listed through the GitHub search API).

**How strong this is.** It is not an official Tesla statement, and the posts are informal congratulations. Even so, a named Reality View engineer and Tesla's self-driving lead both publicly accepted, and in Duan's case celebrated, the claim that Reality View deploys Gaussian splats to the fleet. Neither corrected it. That is far stronger than anything behind the "generative video, not splats" reading. That reading rested on Musk's loose wording and fan explainers, all relayed through search snippets.

**What the demo video shows.** Neda's video (1584×1080, 30 fps, 38 s) was downloaded and examined frame by frame. Evidence frames are in `reports/Tesla Reality View radiance fields media/`.

- **Free orbit far off the camera trajectory.** In the first ~4 s the driver drags a finger across the screen. The virtual camera swings from the chase position to an oblique side view (`orbit_t0-4s.jpg`, `offaxis_t3.5s.jpg`). At ~27.5 s it sits at roughly 90°, looking straight across the street at the house fronts (`side_view_t27.5s.jpg`). No physical camera on the car has that viewpoint, and the geometry stays coherent: parallax, house facades, parked cars. This is the novel-view test the first draft said nobody had demonstrated. It is now demonstrated, and it is what an explicit 3D representation makes cheap.
- **Splat-typical artifacts in the off-axis views.** Foliage looks wispy and semi-transparent, with elongated brush-stroke smears. Dark floaters hang in the sky above the street. Edges are soft and fuzzy on poorly observed surfaces, such as a covered car and distant trees. The ego Model Y stays crisp and looks like a separately rendered asset. These are textbook signs of 3DGS rendered from sparse, forward-moving capture. A video generator could in principle imitate them, but they are exactly what an explicit Gaussian scene looks like from an off-trajectory view.
- **Text and markings stay geometrically consistent.** Painted "STOP" legends and lane arrows stay legible and keep their positions as the camera moves. The opposite lane's STOP legend correctly reads upside-down ("dOTS") from our side of the intersection (`intersection_road_markings.jpg`). This fits real camera pixels anchored in 3D. It does not fit the drifting, garbled text typical of unconstrained video generation.
- **Not established: refresh cadence.** A frame-differencing test for a ~4–5 Hz scene-update rhythm, the signature of Tesla's ~220 ms GGS, came out inconclusive because hand-held phone shake swamps it. A tripod-mounted capture is needed.

**How this fits Musk's "conditional mean-flow model" description.** The first draft treated "generative / mean-flow / AI video generation" and "Gaussian splatting" as mutually exclusive. They are not. Tesla's own October 2025 description of **Generative Gaussian Splatting** says it is *generative*, feed-forward, ~220 ms, handles dynamic objects and is "jointly trained with the end-to-end AI model." A mean-flow (few-step flow-matching) generator conditioned on Universal Vision Encoder features could output Gaussian parameters instead of pixels. The display would then rasterize those Gaussians from any user-chosen camera at full frame rate, while the scene itself refreshes at the model's slower rate. "No game engine" stays true. "Arbitrary virtual camera" in the release notes becomes literal. **This architecture is an inference.** No Tesla source has described the Reality View pipeline at that level. The open sub-questions are whether Gaussians are rasterized on the AI4 NPU or the infotainment GPU, and whether a generative step also fills unobserved regions such as the car's roof and the area behind it.

## Original analysis (first draft, 2026-11-22): sourcing still useful, verdict superseded

The first draft concluded that Tesla's FSD v15 "Reality View" was **not a Gaussian splatting or NeRF renderer**. Every first-party description of the feature calls it a learned generative video model. Tesla's release notes call it a "real-time generative driving visualization." Elon Musk calls it "real-time AI video generation (no gaming engine!)" running on half of one AI4 TRIP neural accelerator, and he describes it as "a conditional mean-flow model, based on the recent sequence of Tesla Universal Vision Encoder" features ([@TeslaSI](https://x.com/TeslaSI/status/2124229676055040007); [news18a](https://english.news18a.com/news/english_316936.html); [Tesla Newswire relaying Musk](https://x.com/TeslaNewswire/status/2124502660670009468)). The first draft said no Tesla employee had used the words "Gaussian," "splat," "NeRF" or "radiance field" about Reality View. *That is no longer true: see Phil Duan's and Shahan Neda's posts above.* No firmware teardown has found splat assets or shaders. The "it's Gaussian splatting" story has two sources. The real one is Ashok Elluswamy's October 2025 ICCV disclosure of a separate **"Generative Gaussian Splatting"** system that Tesla presented for debugging and evaluation. The other is a short chain of forum and blog posts that merged that work with the November 21, 2026 launch. For radiancefields.com the accurate framing is **generative, pose-conditioned novel-view synthesis**: it borders on the radiance-field world but is not a radiance field, and it may be the first neural-rendered driver visualization shipped to consumers. A sourcing caveat applies throughout. Several hosts were blocked during research, including x.com, teslarati.com, notateslaapp.com, electrek.co, kucoin.com and USPTO. ### The first draft's case against splats (now largely answered)

**The evidence against splats is strong and has several independent strands.** First, Tesla's own vocabulary ("generative," "AI video generation," "conditional mean-flow," "world model") all describes networks that output pixels. Second, the compute location matters. Musk places the workload on a TRIP accelerator inside the AI4 FSD computer, which is a matrix-multiply neural engine. He does not place it on the AMD Ryzen/RDNA2 infotainment GPU ([news18a](https://english.news18a.com/news/english_316936.html); [VideoCardz](https://videocardz.com/newz/tesla-car-computer-features-zen-ryzen-embedded-apu-and-discrete-navi-23-gpu)). 3DGS rasterization depends on per-tile depth sorting and alpha compositing, which fit a GPU and fit an NPU poorly. A network that decodes frames fits an NPU naturally. Third, the latency does not match. Tesla's own figure for Generative Gaussian Splatting is **~220 ms**, about 4.5 Hz ([@aelluswamy](https://x.com/aelluswamy/status/1981644831790379245)). The fan-relayed Reality View figures are conditioning updated **18 times per second** and output at **36 fps** in 8-frame chunks ([Tesla Newswire](https://x.com/TeslaNewswire/status/2124502660670009468)). Unless GGS has been sped up roughly fourfold and also moved to a different processor, it cannot be the live per-frame path. Fourth, no teardown has turned up shader names, `.ply`/`.splat`/`.spz` assets, spherical-harmonic buffers or renderer strings for v15, and Tesla has not named an engine or toolchain ([shattered.io](https://shattered.io/musk-fsd-v15-like-gta-reality-view-three-claims-unproven/); [Teslascope v15.0.1](https://teslascope.com/software/full-self-driving/15.0.1)). Fifth, the exposed viewpoint is narrow. Tesla offers pinch-to-zoom and zoom presets on a chase camera "above and behind," and nobody has shown free orbit to a viewpoint far off the camera trajectory ([Teslascope 2026.38.6.11](https://teslascope.com/software/2026.38.6.11); [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/cars/tesla-fsd-v15-rollout/)). A real 3D reconstruction could support arbitrary orbit easily. A generator trained on one family of camera poses could not, which is a reason to keep the camera on a leash.

Informed observers have not seen splats either. Bilawal Sidhu, a spatial-computing commentator who posts 3DGS work regularly, called it "a fully generated 360 drone view trailing your car" that "fuses all the cameras." He said Tesla "goes the opposite direction using no game engine at all," and that it replaces "all the explicit signals and draw calls with one end to end network." He did not mention splats ([Sidhu on X](https://x.com/bilawalsidhu/status/2124323235063816204); [Threads](https://www.threads.com/@bilawal.ai/post/DgBs2U5iBtT/video-teslas-reality-view-handily-sets-a-new-standard-for-spatial-awareness-in/)). No named graphics or radiance-field researcher has published a frame-level analysis that claims to find floaters, anisotropic needles or SH view-dependence. Searches also found no Reddit, Hacker News or r/GaussianSplatting forensic thread.

**The case for a splat or radiance-field link is circumstantial, but it should be stated fairly.** Tesla does run a Gaussian splatting system, and Elluswamy says it "can be jointly trained with the end-to-end AI model" ([@aelluswamy](https://x.com/aelluswamy/status/1981644831790379245)). Reality View reportedly draws on features from that same end-to-end stack. Tesla keeps hiring for Gaussian splatting skills. Self-Driving 3D vision roles cite NeRF/3DGS and novel view synthesis, and a 2026 "AI Engineer, World Modeling & Video Generation" listing reportedly mentions "Gaussian Splatting and volumetric rendering" ([radiancefields.com job board](https://radiancefields.com/job-board/3d-computer-vision-engineer-self-driving); [simplify.jobs](https://simplify.jobs/p/cd599905-4fc3-4bf2-b338-0ffb42e26576/AI-Engineer)). Not a Tesla App's account, in which pixels are mapped "onto a 3D stage" and generative AI then fills the gaps, implies some 3D scaffold ([Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/5065/tesla-fsd-v15-rolls-out-with-incredible-new-reality-view-and-25x-better-safety-video)). Hybrid designs, in which Gaussians provide geometric consistency and diffusion paints unseen regions, are an active 2026 research pattern, for example Xiaomi's WorldRec plus WorldGen and DGGT's diffusion refinement ([arXiv 2605.18137](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.18137v2); [DGGT, CVPR 2026](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Chen_DGGT_Feedforward_4D_Reconstruction_of_Dynamic_Driving_Scenes_using_Unposed_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf)). Musk's "~pixel-perfect world model" for synthetic data could also have been trained partly on GGS reconstructions. None of these points shows splats in the in-car display path. Together they mean only that **a 3D-aware latent or an occupancy/depth scaffold under the generator cannot be ruled out from public information.**

**First-draft verdict (superseded):** it called Reality View "highly likely" not to be an explicit 3DGS/NeRF scene. The new evidence above answers most of the points in this section. The free-orbit test is now demonstrated. The latency mismatch dissolves if Gaussians are generated at ~4–5 Hz and rasterized at display rate. "Generative" and "mean-flow" fit *Generative* Gaussian Splatting. A Tesla self-driving lead and a Reality View engineer publicly endorsed the splat framing. Two points remain open: no firmware teardown has yet found splat assets or shaders, and Tesla has made no formal statement.

### The 2025 Generative Gaussian Splatting disclosure (now the likely engine, not a confusion)

The mix-up has a real origin. On **2025-10-24**, alongside his ICCV 2025 keynote at the WDFM-AD workshop in Honolulu, Elluswamy published an X article, "Tesla's approach to Autonomy." In it he wrote that conventional 3DGS "depends on camera views with large baseline for good performance. Typical vehicle motion, unfortunately, is quite linear." He added that it requires "good initialization from other pipelines and the total optimization time can be on the order of 10s of minutes." He then said: **"Tesla's Generative Gaussian Splatting on the other hand has great generalization, runs in ~220 ms, doesn't require initialization, can model dynamic objects and can be jointly trained with the end-to-end AI model"** ([@aelluswamy X article](https://x.com/aelluswamy/status/1981644831790379245); [full talk video post](https://x.com/aelluswamy/status/1981742083129159687?lang=en)). Write-ups of the talk place the splats under **debugging and evaluation**, as a way to inspect in 3D what the driving model understands. They describe a separate **learned neural world simulator**, an action-conditioned multi-camera video generator, which Tesla uses for closed-loop evaluation and adversarial event injection ([Medium summary](https://medium.com/@byaman019/a-peek-into-teslas-autonomous-future-core-tech-revealed-by-vp-ashok-elluswamy-at-iccv-2025-906df7ae95de); [iTiger](https://www.itiger.com/news/1146546403); [NextBigFuture](https://www.nextbigfuture.com/2025/10/tesla-vp-ashok-describes-technology-of-fsd.html)). Tesla therefore described two parallel technologies: generative 3DGS for reconstruction, and generative video for simulation. Musk's "much smaller version" of the "~pixel-perfect world model" line connects Reality View to the **video world-model line**, not the splat line.

As Tesla describes it, GGS is a feed-forward network that predicts Gaussians from driving clips. That puts it in the same family as STORM (0.18 s on an A100), DGGT (0.39 s) and NVIDIA's Instant NuRec (~1–1.5 s per 10–20 s multi-camera clip, versus ~75 minutes for per-scene optimization) ([STORM arXiv 2501.00602](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.00602); [DGGT](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Chen_DGGT_Feedforward_4D_Reconstruction_of_Dynamic_Driving_Scenes_using_Unposed_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf); [Instant NuRec](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/sil/projects/instant-nurec/)). Tesla's 220 ms figure sits right in that datacenter range. The excerpts do not say whether the figure is per scene, per clip or per frame, or what hardware it was measured on. The work also continues a line that goes back years. At CVPR 2022 Elluswamy wrote "Occupancy Networks ♥️ NeRFs," noting that occupancy "allow[s] for differentiable rendering of images" but, unlike per-scene NeRFs, "generalize[s] across scenes" ([Tesmanian](https://www.tesmanian.com/blogs/tesmanian-blog/tesla-found-a-solution-to-general-obstacle-detection-with-the-use-of-occupancy-networks)). At AI Day 2022 a Tesla presenter called NeRF "his personal weekend project," and Tesla showed an "early result of a 3D reconstruction from our [fleet] data" that prioritized geometry over RGB fidelity ([AI Day 2022 transcript gist](https://gist.github.com/L0rdCha0s/de22ae0c7e7a7a70b37ac9c1262e27e1)). Tesla has been working with radiance-field ideas for four years. It has never presented them as an in-car display technology.

The specific conflation can be traced through four posts. A Japanese owner forum linked Reality View to "3D Gaussian Splatting at 220ms intervals" from a "CVPR26" keynote. The 220 ms number is the ICCV 2025 figure, so the forum either misattributed it or Tesla repeated the material at Elluswamy's CVPR 2026 keynote, which is unconfirmed ([teskas.net](https://teskas.net/t/fsd-v15-ui-gta/44161); [Basenor on CVPR 2026](https://www.basenor.com/blogs/news/teslas-ashok-elluswamy-heads-to-cvpr-2026-for-ai-showdown)). A KuCoin news flash relayed a Chinese blogger's claim that Tesla "achieved a 3D Gaussian full-scene reconstruction despite hardware disadvantages." The same flash repeats Musk's "AI video, no game engine" line, which contradicts the blogger ([KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/tesla-fsd-v15-uses-half-of-ai4-chip-s-trip-accelerator-for-real-time-ai-video)). Weibo posts describing "real-time AI modeling of 3D street scenes" (实时AI建模3D街景) added ambiguity but no forensics ([Weibo](https://weibo.com/2/detail/5368052469728925)). The most widely shared fan explainer, @tslaming, reportedly **corrected an earlier version** of its own breakdown to say Tesla "did demo generative Gaussian splatting at ICCV, just not as the engine behind this view" ([@tslaming](https://x.com/tslaming/status/2124474912161087636)). Name collisions make it easier to go wrong. Meta has an unrelated paper also titled "Generative Gaussian Splatting" ([katjaschwarz.github.io/ggs](https://katjaschwarz.github.io/ggs)), and a NeurIPS 2026 4DGS paper called "TESLA" has no apparent tie to Tesla Inc. ([NeurIPS poster](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/poster/152766)). No major Tesla outlet in the results (Electrek, Not a Tesla App, Drive Tesla Canada, Teslarati) claimed Reality View uses splats.

### Industry context: possibly the first splat-based driver display shipped to consumers

Across the AV industry, radiance fields and splats are standard in **offline simulation and data tooling**. Examples include Waymo's Block-NeRF and EmerNeRF lineage, NVIDIA's 3DGRT/3DGUT-based Omniverse NuRec, Wayve's PRISM-1 4DGS for Ghost Gym, Waabi's UniSim and Xiaomi's WorldRec. Since 2025 the frontier has moved toward **generative video world models**: Waymo's Genie 3-based World Model, NVIDIA OmniDreams, Wayve GAIA-2 and Tesla's own neural simulator ([Waymo blog](https://waymo.com/blog/2026/02/the-waymo-world-model-a-new-frontier-for-autonomous-driving-simulation); [NVIDIA OmniDreams](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/sil/projects/omnidreams-blog/); [CnEVPost on Xiaomi](https://cnevpost.com/2026/05/26/xiaomi-ev-introduces-world-model/)). The research found no automaker that had shipped a splat- or NeRF-rendered driver visualization before v15. The nearest case is Xiaomi's vague May 2026 claim that its Gaussian-based world model serves "smart cabin systems." Reality View therefore looks like the first consumer deployment of a **neural-rendered** in-car view. It is a world-model distillation, not a radiance-field deployment.

Feasibility points the same way. Rendering splats in a car is easy: Mobile-GS reaches 116–127 FPS on a Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 phone GPU, and newer Model S/X carry a Navi 23 GPU in the RX 6600 class ([Mobile-GS](https://xiaobiaodu.github.io/mobile-gs-project/); [VideoCardz](https://videocardz.com/newz/tesla-car-computer-features-zen-ryzen-embedded-apu-and-discrete-navi-23-gpu)). The hard part is continuous feed-forward reconstruction from eight cameras at 10 Hz or more on a fraction of one automotive NPU. No published driving-3DGS model has been benchmarked on an automotive SoC, and streaming reconstruction is less mature than per-clip reconstruction ([DrivingScene arXiv 2510.24734](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.24734)). The chase-cam viewpoint also runs into the known off-trajectory sparsity problem of driving reconstructions ([MCSplat](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.25345)). The area above and behind the car, and the car's own roof, are never observed, so a splat scene would show holes or smeared ellipsoids there unless something generative fills them. A generator fills those regions by design. That is a strong engineering reason for Tesla to have chosen the generative route for this particular viewpoint.

## Conclusion

The rumour was closer to right than the first draft was. Taken together, the evidence now points to Gaussian splatting inside Reality View. A named Reality View engineer with a public splat-renderer portfolio accepted the claim that he is deploying "splats" to millions of Teslas. Tesla's self-driving lead called him "a gaussian splats champion" on launch day. The shipped feature lets drivers orbit the camera to viewpoints no physical camera has, and shows the floaters and anisotropic smears that 3DGS produces off-trajectory. The most coherent reading of all first-party statements is a generative, feed-forward Gaussian splatting model (Tesla's ICCV 2025 GGS line, likely a mean-flow variant conditioned on the Universal Vision Encoder). It produces a live splat scene that is rasterized from a user-controllable virtual camera. If confirmed, this is a landmark for the field: the first radiance-field representation shipped as a primary, always-on driver display, across a fleet of millions of cars.

Three things are needed before publishing this as fact. First, an on-record Tesla confirmation: ask Neda, Duan or Elluswamy directly whether Reality View rasterizes Gaussians and where. Second, a firmware teardown that finds Gaussian buffers or splat-rasterization code. Third, a tripod-mounted capture to measure the scene-refresh cadence against the ~220 ms GGS figure. Until then the defensible framing is this: *Tesla engineers publicly tie Reality View to Gaussian splatting, and the footage behaves like a live, generatively predicted splat scene.* Musk's "AI video generation" wording describes the generative model, not the absence of splats.
